# teightysix article: what I need from you before I can draft

Recording script, 2026-09-29. Companion to
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-teightysix-article-design.md`.

## How to answer

Answer by number. Voice memo, typed, in any order, across as many sittings as you
like. Nothing here needs a paragraph; a phrase is usually better.

Three rules that make the answers usable:

1. **Concrete nouns beat adjectives.** "A green Xacto and a self-healing mat" is
   usable. "Some cutting tools" is not. If you can name the object, name it.
2. **"I don't remember" is a real answer and I want it.** It is strictly better than
   a guess, because a guess becomes a sentence with your name on it that turns out to
   be wrong. Say "skip" and I will either cut the detail or write around it.
3. **Do not summarize for me.** Say the thing the way you would say it to someone
   sitting next to you. The point of this exercise is to get your sentences, not
   your notes. The messy version is the one I want.

Where a question asks whether a framing works, "no" is genuinely useful. I would
rather cut a neat line than publish one you would not have said.

**Tiers.** **[BLOCKING]** means I cannot draft that beat without it and will not
invent around it. **[SHAPING]** changes how a beat is told but I can draft a version
without it. **[OPTIONAL]** would make a section better and costs you nothing to skip.
